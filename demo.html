zecor console · a demo

Flying the console

The autonomous build fleet used to be markdown files in folders and a cron job. zecor console is the cockpit — and it is not a dashboard with a command line. The centre of the screen is a conversation with the fleet: it answers questions, it asks you things when a card is stuck, and the whole exchange survives a reconnect. The board moved to a rail.

zecor console· needs the console extra: uv sync --group console

The conversation

You talk to the fleet in plain language. A control verb — stop, retry 0043 with opus, assign 0047 cc2-layer2 — is caught by a closed, no-AI grammar and runs immediately; a rate-limited request must never sit between you and stop. Anything else — "why is 0043 stuck?", "draft two cards for the deploy checkpoint" — goes to a frontier model on a worker thread. It can propose actions; you confirm each. If the frontier is unreachable it says Layer-2 offline and everything else keeps working.

Every turn is one row in SQLite. Kill the console, reopen it — over mosh from a trailhead, or fresh after a reboot — and the conversation replays. A background watchd writes narration and questions into the same log with no console attached, so nothing that happens while you're gone is lost.

It asks you things

When a card lands in blocked / needs-human-design / gate-broken, the fleet posts a question addressed to you — keyed by the card and its blocking reason, so a poll loop or a restart never reposts it. Your plain-text reply becomes the retry note and re-dispatches the card in one move. The answer is marked done before the retry fires, so two clients can't double-dispatch, and a failed retry re-opens the question instead of swallowing it.

The roadmap, rolled up live

Mission › Campaign › Move › Card. No status is stored: every node's roll-up is recomputed from the live cards under it, worst-state-wins, each time you press m. This is the machine's own plan — Run for a week unattended and The console flies like a chat among the campaigns. A Move with no cards is UNPLANNED; when the queue drains, replenish decomposes the next unplanned Move into cards and the loop keeps going.

Is it working?

M is land rate by tier, cycle time, a rough cost estimate and a daily-lands sparkline — read from the attempt ledger. It says "no runs yet" in plain words until the ledger fills. Built and tested against synthetic rows; tuned after the first real run.

From your pocket

The layout is responsive. Below ~100 columns — a phone over mosh — the board and panes fold away and the chat is the whole screen, but every action stays reachable through the grammar and the keys: STOP, retry, answer a question, open any pane full-screen. Above ~140 the roadmap and a mini output pane sit alongside the chat.

The remote path today is Tailscale through carrier NAT + mosh for signal loss + tmux for the disconnect. zecor remote setup does that install in one command; a responsive web view on the tailnet (zecor serve + the PWA under webui/) covers a pocket glance with no terminal app.
